**Honest scope caveat**: this is a headless microbenchmark of the Time & Tick System's own compute

`vox-018`'s own approach, out of reach for a single headless GdUnit4 suite). This system's own cost is
population-independent (the formula is O(1) regardless of villager count), so the measurement is a
valid real proxy for TR-044's combined-cost claim, but it does not itself certify the full-build,
20–30-villager scenario the story's Engine Notes describe. Flagged explicitly rather than silently
implied as full integrated-build verification — consistent with this sprint's own "honest PASS, not
assumed" discipline (see the vox-018 PASS/MISS section of the QA plan for the sibling precedent).

## 5. Quick-spec AC4 — 20x warp margin, catch-up cap behavior at 12 (BLOCKING per QA plan)
